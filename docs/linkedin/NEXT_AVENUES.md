## What is popular/useful (2026 LinkedIn data, all sources cited)

- Document carousels (PDF): ~7% engagement, highest of any format, +14%
  YoY; win on dwell time; best for frameworks/tutorials.
  (gallium.ai/blog/linkedin-content-formats-2026; metricool via tonemark.ai)
- Text posts: workhorse, most comments/DMs, best for POV/stories.
  2–5x/week, Tue–Thu 10–11am. (conbersa.ai, sproutworth.com, thoughtleadership.app)
- Native video 30–90s: 5x conversations, growing distribution, highest
  trust transfer. (conbersa.ai)
- Build-in-public with REAL numbers: most credible format; milestones,
  failures, metrics. (conbersa.ai, gallopeer.com, foundera.co)
- Contrarian takes + "I was wrong" updates: highest-yield authority
  formats. (foundera.co template library)
- Definition posts: 2–3% engagement + SEO/AI-search positioning.
  (gallopeer.com)
- LinkedIn is the #2 most-cited source in AI search; 72% of citations
  are text posts; 51% go to accounts under 10k followers — expertise and
  consistency beat audience size. Posting 2–3x/week is the citation
  sweet spot. (Meltwater 2026 report via learn.meltwater.com)
- Constraints: external links in body = 0.5–0.8x reach — links go in
  comments, never the post. Polls: conflicting data (200%+ reach vs
  algorithmic deprioritization) — use sparingly, for research not growth.
  Stories (relaunched 2026): atmosphere only, never substance.
- The market thesis is live: "AI verification gap" — 81% of tech leaders
  report more prod failures from AI code despite 92% pre-deploy
  confidence. (steezr.com, 2026-06-17)
